1. Train & evaluate

ESC-50: 50 categories. Folds 1–3 train, fold 4 validates and fold 5 tests. Two validation clips sharing test-source IDs are excluded; test fold 5 stays intact. Windows never cross the resulting source-separated splits. This is a single holdout, not a five-fold benchmark.

Dataset download is about 600 MB. Training runs on your computer, outside the request timeout. Keep this page open for progress.

Training runs locally. This hosted page serves the exported model and results; it does not train in a serverless request.

What this demonstrates

The classifier learns from real labelled sounds. DEDA uses actual model scores with explicit dataset class indices. Configured warning categories include horn, siren, dog, glass, alarm clock, knocking and crying baby. Rain has an information cue, not a danger designation. Other recognized sounds are shown without automatically declaring a hazard. Low confidence can withhold a warning.

Mono audio does not reveal source direction. The default uses general four-output cues. The optional virtual-array mode calculates a simulated angle and labels it explicitly; it does not claim recorded spatial evidence. Recognition needs 1,024 ms of audio before its first prediction, then updates every 256 ms. This does not meet a 300 ms sound-onset target. Clip labels are not exact onset annotations.

Scores are model estimates, not correctness guarantees. Uploaded audio is outside the reported test set. These new recognition results are separate from the paper's synthetic decision-stream results and do not establish wearable or user benefit.

ESC-50 / Freesound audio is used for noncommercial educational evaluation. Dataset licenses and original creator credits.
